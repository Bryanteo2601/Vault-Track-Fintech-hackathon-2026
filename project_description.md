# 🛡️ VaultTrack- The Ultimate Wealth Wellness Hub for the Modern Singaporean

---
## Project Description
VaultTrack by FintechG0Ns is a comprehensive *Wealth Wellness Hub* designed to unify fragmented financial ecosystems into a single, actionable Wealth Wallet. To eliminate data obscurity, VaultTrack aggregates accounts from banks, investments, loans, and insurance, alongside private assets and Singapore-specific CPF data, delivering a truly holistic, real-time view of an investor’s total financial health.

---

## The Solution
We believe the true goal of financial planning isn’t just about hitting "financial freedom," but ensuring our clients have the resources to actually enjoy their retirement. Tailored specifically for the Singapore market, VaultTrack solves the complexity gap with a proprietary Wealth Wellness Score and an in-depth CPF Financial Planner featuring automated payout estimators and retirement milestone tracking through BRS, FRS, and ERS. 

Furthermore, the platform performs 10,000-run Monte Carlo simulations and scenario-based stress testing to identify potential financial risks before they materialize, allowing users to understand portfolio performance under diverse market conditions. This is augmented by a built-in Gemini AI chatbot that provides structured recommendation insights, enabling users to make more informed, data-driven financial decisions.

---

## Technical UX
Built on a type-safe tRPC, React Native, and Drizzle ORM stack, the app ensures a robust, "offline-first" experience with secure Firebase cloud synchronization. The UX employs progressive disclosure, moving users intuitively from high-level wellness gauges to granular CBS-style credit breakdowns and TDSR calculators.

---

## Impact & Market
By localizing complex regulatory frameworks, VaultTrack provides a commercially viable, scalable solution that enhances financial literacy and proactive engagement for the modern Singaporean investor.
